# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated explicitly in the master brief: modern frontend supported by the environment. Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript and Vercel deployment. The original accepted design is preserved during runtime migration.

## Users

Arabic-speaking shop owners and businesses in Baghdad comparing printing, vehicle wrapping and signage suppliers and requesting a quotation.

## Product Purpose

A complete Arabic digital presence for the fictional مطبعة المؤمل للدعاية والإعلان. Visitors understand capabilities, inspect illustrative work, and prepare a project inquiry.

## Positioning

A practical Baghdad production workshop connecting design, materials, print and installation. No invented awards, certified claims or major clients.

## Operating Context

Commercial signage, printed posters, glass films, portraits, indoor neon, vehicle graphics, LED displays. Quote needs depend on measurements, materials, quantity and installation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Home, company profile, seven service details, portfolio with detail pages, quote and contact forms, FAQ, privacy, terms, cookie policy and consent controls, custom 404. Arabic RTL, responsive, accessible, SEO. Mock contact and portfolio data are centralized. Forms run a clearly disclosed local simulation; no transmission or backend claims. Files can be chosen locally but are not uploaded. No analytics or marketing scripts enabled.

## Brand Commitments

The user delegates every creative decision and explicitly says not to ask them to choose. Premium, contemporary, commercial, believable Baghdad context; rich original photography and strong Arabic type; no SaaS style, excessive gradients, pills or repetitive card grids.

## Evidence on Hand

User master brief. All business details, projects and testimonials are explicitly fictional and labeled accordingly. Original generated photography depicts illustrative projects, not completed customer jobs.

## Product Principles

Show the actual service and material. Make quotation requirements clear. Use useful Iraqi Arabic. Keep mock functionality honest. Complete every route and state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic RTL Arabic, keyboard operation, visible focus, contrast, reduced motion, readable text and touch targets. Responsive desktop/tablet/mobile.
